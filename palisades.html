Palisades
Drive In

This project focuses on redesigning the Palisades Drive Inn website. The goal was to create a clearer, more engaging experience that better showcases the restaurant's offerings while addressing the firm's outdated and difficult to navigate online presence. Through research and design processes, the following issues were addressed in Adobe InDesign based on a specific format required by the course.

01

Design Brief

For this project, a design brief was established to gain information on the company's background summary, target audience, competition & content, and project overview. From research, Palisades Drive Inn is a long-standing Pearl City restaurant serving plate lunches, catering, and takeout. With competition from local brands like L&L Hawaiian Barbecue, the business needs a refreshed identity and website for nearby students and families. As a result, this brief establishes the need to modernize the brand, improve usability, and enhance the firm's online experience.

02

User Persona Demographics

After researching Palisades Drive Inn's customer base, I developed user personas to clarify who the restaurant serves and how they interact with the brand. These profiles helped identify key touch points, pain points, and opportunities across both online and in-person experiences associated with the company. This groundwork guided the direction of the redesign—ensuring that every feature, layout choice, and content decision supports user needs, improves satisfaction, and strengthens overall engagement.

03

Functional Specifications

To create an effective user experience, the website redesign required defining key functional sections. The consists of focusing on four essential pages: About Us, Menu, Catering, and Contact. Each page serves a specific purpose, such as introducing the brand and its values, showcasing food offerings with high-quality visuals, outlining catering services, and providing information. These specifications create a strong foundation that improves navigation and supports the restaurant's goals.

04

Sitemap

The sitemap outlines the website's core structure. This includes the firm's Home page leading to four key subpages, which consists of About Us, Menu, Catering, and Contact. Each page serves a clear purpose, such as sharing the restaurant's story, previewing menu items, highlighting catering options, and providing essential business information such as hours, location, and contact details. Establishing this sitemap structure is necessary to ensure straightforward navigation and a user-friendly experience.

05

Style Tile

The style tile establishes the visual direction for the redesigned website. It defines the color palette, typography, imagery, and interface elements that shape the brand's overall look and feel. The selected colors and reference images reflect the restaurant's heritage and atmosphere, while the typography sets a clear hierarchy for titles, headings, and body content. Icons, buttons, and logo treatments are also included to guide interaction and reinforce consistency across the site. These elements are used to help establish the visual direction of the site.

06

Final Wireframes

The final wireframe brings together the site's main pages. The Home page introduces the restaurant and guides visitors toward key sections. The About Us page highlights the restaurant's history, mission, and values to build trust and reinforce its role in the community. The Menu page organizes food offerings with high-quality visuals and clear descriptions, while the Catering page outlines services for events and large orders. This establishes a modern structure that improves navigation and engagement, while fulfilling business and user goals.

Reflection

Redesigning the website allowed me to address user and business challenges. Research and user personas revealed issues such as unclear navigation and outdated visuals. The wireframes solved these problems with clearer structure, organized content, and high-quality imagery that help customers find information quickly. For the business, this redesign modernizes the brand, strengthens storytelling, and improves visibility for core services, which supports higher engagement, better usability, and a more competitive digital presence. This shows how design can meet user expectations while supporting business goals.
