About Me

Projects

Every project has a story. While developing websites or advertisements, I prioritize purposeful visual design that aligns with business and audience needs. By applying marketing concepts and design principles, these projects showcases my approach to solving problems with purpose.

01

Palisades Drive-In: Website Redesign

Palisades Drive-In is famous for its food products. However, its web presence was outdated, difficult to navigate, and lacked the narrative depth that makes the physical restaurant a community anchor. This project aimed to redesign a new website for Palisades Drive-In that honors the rich history, community significance, and cultural legacy of the company, while capturing the spirit that defines the business.

02

Harley Davidson: Print Advertisement

Harley-Davidson is an iconic motorcycle manufacturer, known for its deep American roots and brand identity built around freedom, individuality, and an open-road lifestyle. This project examines the process of creating a printed Harley-Davidson advertisement and explores how using design and marketing concepts contributes to the advertisements effectiveness in achieving business and user goals.
